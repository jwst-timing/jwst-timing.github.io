Instruments

Fast timing with JWST relies on reading out small detector subarrays, which shortens frame times to a fraction of a second.

MIRI

The Mid-Infrared Instrument (5–28 µm). Used for our spectral-timing studies of X-ray binaries, including GRS 1915+105, GX 339–4 and V404 Cyg.

  • MIRI detector subarrays
  • New MIRI subarrays for Cycle 6, read out in phase with the 390 Hz EMI noise

NIRCam

The Near-Infrared Camera (0.6–5 µm). Used for the clock calibration and for stellar occultations.

  • NIRCam detector subarrays
  • NIRCam superstripe subarrays for imaging and time-series imaging (new in Cycle 6)
